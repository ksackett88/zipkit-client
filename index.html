Design Intake

Panel Kit Project — Checklist & Plan Review

This packet includes a Design Intake Checklist and standard Zip Kit plans for you to review and mark up. Together, these help our design team understand your priorities before design work begins.

Redline the Plans

Focus on layout and structure.
  • Room layout and sizes
  • Bedroom / bathroom count
  • Ceiling heights or vaults
  • Garage or basement intent
  • Window and door locations (concept-level)

Skip for Now

Handled later in selections phase.
  • Flooring
  • Cabinets
  • Paint, tile, or décor
  • Furniture placement

Step 1: Download the Standard Plans

Pick the floor plan(s) closest to what you want, download the PDF, and mark them up. Note the plan name in Section 2 below.

What to Do

  1. Complete the Design Intake Checklist in full. Missing information may delay the start of design.
  2. Mark up the included plans to show what you like, what you want changed, and what doesn't work for you.

Next Steps

Design begins once we receive your completed checklist and redlined plans. Our team will review your input and contact you with the next steps. Clear input now keeps your design moving smoothly.

Building Systems & Coordination

12. Building Envelope — Required

UPCHARGES: Continuously Insulated (CI) System is an upgrade over Standard Sheathing — adds material cost, detailing, and coordination complexity, but significantly improves energy performance.

13. Domestic Hot Water System Preference

FOR COORDINATION: Water heater is installed by your GC. Zip Kit needs to know the type to size and locate mechanical space on the panel kit.

14. Primary Heating & Cooling System — Required

FOR COORDINATION: Zip Kit does not design or supply HVAC. Final equipment selection is by a licensed mechanical contractor. However, HVAC approach affects framing, soffits, chases, and ceiling design — early selection is required for design coordination.

18. Design Assumption Defaults

If selections are left undecided, Zip Kit Homes will proceed with the following reasonable, code-compliant assumptions for your panel kit design:

  • Standard ceiling heights (9')
  • Standard window sizing and placements

  • No garage
